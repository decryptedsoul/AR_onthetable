# Coconut Grove website concept

Implementation update: the layout is now rendered by [React components](./frontend/src/Layout.tsx) with the menu supplied by the [FastAPI backend](./backend/app/main.py). See [architecture](./ARCHITECTURE.md) and [setup](./README.md). The original design argument below remains the visual baseline.

## Design argument

- A guest at a table wants to browse food quickly, then understand a serving before choosing.
- First screen: Coconut Grove is a restaurant on Tipu Sultan Road, Karachi. Its own published interior photograph establishes the place; the menu action is immediate.
- Menu: six explicitly illustrative dishes demonstrate category browsing, search, saves, and detailed portion information.
- Table preview: explain the planned experience and show a clear route to its requirements, without simulating real camera tracking.
- Visit: link to the supplied official Instagram account for current restaurant information.

## Subject inventory and idea

Coconut Grove; CG palm identity; green and gold; warm ivory; Tipu Sultan Road; Karachi; illuminated arches; open-air dining; patterned floor; greenery; menu viewed while sitting at a table.

On this page, Coconut Grove's illuminated arches become a persistent view of the restaurant beside a digital menu, like reading the menu while sitting in the room.

The live interaction is exploring one dish's serving, ingredients, and spice information from the menu. Favorites and search support that task.

Direction: split-stage. Keep the existing four-color palette. A serif display face paired with a system sans is a proposed type treatment, not official brand typography. Use the available restaurant photo and representative stock food photos. Replace the dealt founder's letter with a facts-and-copy section: no founder statement has been supplied. No invented testimonials, rating aggregates, or claims of live AR.

## Honest prototype boundaries

All dish descriptions, prices, recipes, preparation times, portions and spice values are sample data. Photos illustrate the dish category and are not verified photographs of restaurant servings. Reviews have an empty state and an explicitly local demo submission. No booking, ordering, payment, live availability, camera tracking or 3D assets are provided.

Reference interior: https://foodoplanet.com/listing/coconut-grove/ (restaurant promotional reference; permission and original high-resolution file required for production).

Sample food images from Unsplash (source URLs also listed in README). They illustrate the concept only. No video was supplied; the page uses restrained interface animation rather than unrelated footage.
